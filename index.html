Invoices made from your own data. Reminders follow.

Customers, orders and time reports from Fortnox or Visma become invoices: number, OCR, VAT, rotavdrag, due date. Then the bank file is matched, and every overdue invoice gets a reminder draft in Swedish or English. A person approves each draft. Nothing is sent by the machine.

How it works

1 · Make invoices

Every finished order: rows and reported hours, discount, VAT on the sum, rotavdrag or reverse charge, number, OCR, due date. Anything unsure goes to a person.

2 · Match

The bank file: OCR first, then invoice numbers in the message. Anything unclear goes to a person.

3 · Find late

What is left on each invoice, how many days late, which tone: friendly, firmer or last notice.

4 · Draft

One reminder per invoice. A check proves every number in it equals the books.

Swedish rules it follows

What an invoice must show

Date, a unique number in one series, both parties' names and addresses, our VAT number, what was done, net per rate, VAT rate and amount. No gaps: an order a person must check gets no number.

Mervärdesskattelag (2023:200) 17 kap. 24–25 §§

VAT 25 %, on the sum

Moms is worked out once on the net sum, not per row, so the öre add up. Normal rate 25 %.

Mervärdesskattelag (2023:200) 9 kap. 2 §

Rotavdrag: 30 % of the work

Only work, with moms; not material or travel. At most 50 000 kr per person and year. Needs personnummer and fastighetsbeteckning. Rounded down to whole kronor.

skatteverket.se: så fungerar rotavdraget

Reverse charge for builders

Building work sold to a builder: no VAT, the words "Omvänd betalningsskyldighet" and the buyer's VAT number. Goods only: normal VAT.

skatteverket.se: omvänd betalningsskyldighet inom byggsektorn

Reminder fee: 60 kr, only if agreed

Only when the customer agreed to it, at the latest when the debt arose. Otherwise no fee.

Lag (1981:739) 2 § and 4 §

Interest: referensränta + 8

Dröjsmålsränta runs from the due date, on what is left each day. Referensränta 2,00 % from 1 July 2026, so 10 % per year.

Räntelagen (1975:635) 3 §, 6 §, 9 § · riksbank.se

What it does not do

  • It does not decide if a job is building work or rot work. The order says so; the code checks the facts that order needs and stops if one is missing.
  • The 50 000 kr rot limit is counted on our own invoices only. The customer may have used rot with another firm this year.
  • If Skatteverket refuses the rotavdrag, the customer owes that part. A person sends that invoice; the demo does not.
  • Credit invoices, sales of goods to other countries and VAT rates other than 25 % go to a person.
  • The number check guards numbers, not grammar. A person reads every reminder before it goes out.
  • It sends nothing. Invoices and reminders wait as drafts for a person.

Run it yourself

python3 -m unittest            # 29 checks, no model needed
python3 make_data.py           # the sample export: customers, articles, orders, time reports, bank file
python3 run.py                 # 77 orders -> invoices -> bank file -> reminders; score card, updates this page

The firm, customers, orders and payments are made up. Personnummer are Skatteverket's published test numbers; org and VAT numbers have a wrong check digit on purpose, so none is a real firm's. Results on this page come from one real run on a laptop. This is not legal or tax advice.
